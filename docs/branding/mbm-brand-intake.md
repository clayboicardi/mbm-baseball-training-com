set the **aesthetic**: a Día de Muertos emblem — central decorated skull, two

**Reconciled direction (Myles confirmed "literal Día de Muertos baseball"):**

| Reference element | → MBM emblem element |

| central sugar skull | **baseball styled as a sugar-skull / calavera**, with the **"11"** on it (present, not focal) |
| two flanking guitars | **two baseball bats crossed in an "X"** |

## 5. Style anchor — restrained Día de Muertos folk-art sports emblem

A **calavera baseball badge** in the clean folk-art graphic register (sugar-skull /
papel-picado): bold outlines, **flat solid color fills**, symmetrical ornament,
festive but disciplined.

- **Allowed / wanted:** sugar-skull facial motifs on the ball, marigold + papel-
  picado flourishes, ornate symmetry, a diamond frame, a bottom banner ribbon,
  lively energy.
- **Forbidden:** photoreal, 3D-render, painterly/watercolor, muddy gradients, glow,
  drop-shadow soup. Ornate is welcome; **noisy/blurry is not** — detail stays
  clean, symmetrical, hierarchical, and **vectorizable**.
- **Original work:** traditional public-domain calavera/marigold motifs only. Do
  NOT trace the specific stock reference, and do NOT copy any trademarked team /
  MLB / college / Day-of-the-Dead jersey art.

## 6. Logo SYSTEM (a family, not one image)

1. **Primary emblem** — full ornate calavera-baseball badge (diamond frame, crossed
   bats, flares, bottom banner, "11" on the ball).
2. **Simplified icon** — favicon/avatar mark, legible at **16–32px**. *Separate,
   drastically simpler derivative.*
3. **Wordmark** — "MBM BASEBALL TRAINING" (and an "MBM" short form) in an athletic type.
4. **Lockups** — horizontal, stacked, emblem-alone, wordmark-alone.

### Icon-reduction strategy (decision)

The ornate emblem won't survive 16px — the icon is its own design.
- **A (CC recommended): baseball + "11"** — clean ball, "11", maybe a 2-stroke hint
  of eye sockets/stitching. Most legible tiny; safest favicon/IG-avatar.
- **B: minimal skull-ball glyph** — a reduced calavera face on the ball; on-theme,
  riskier at 16px.
- **C: "MBM" monogram** — strong alt avatar / merch mark.

Lean **A** for favicon; explore **B** and the **C** monogram in Pass 2–3 as alternates.

```
You generate brand logo/identity assets for "MBM Baseball Training," a private

Long Beach CA. Audience: players ages 8–18 and their parents. Feel: energetic,
confident, trustworthy, with vibrant cultural personality. Register: a sports/
athletic EMBLEM rendered in a restrained Día de Muertos ("Day of the Dead")

SUBJECT (the primary emblem):
- A baseball-DIAMOND-shaped badge. Centered: a baseball styled with sugar-skull

  beneath the ball. Decorative folk-art flares (marigold flowers / papel-picado
  flourishes). A banner ribbon across the bottom reading "MBM BASEBALL TRAINING".
- The number "11" appears somewhere ON the baseball — present but NOT the focal

PALETTE (strict, exact hexes):
- Dodger Blue #005A9C and White #FFFFFF must VISUALLY DOMINATE every composition.
- Red #EF3E42 as a small accent. A single warm marigold-gold accent (~#F2B705) is
  permitted, used SPARINGLY for festive folk-art detail. No other colors unless I
  explicitly ask.

STYLE:
- Crisp, FLAT, high-contrast folk-art vector — clean papel-picado / sugar-skull
  graphic register: bold outlines, solid color fills, symmetrical ornament.
- NOT photoreal, NOT 3D-rendered, NOT painterly/watercolor; no muddy gradients, no
  glow, no drop-shadow soup. Ornate is welcome; noisy/blurry is not. Keep detail
  clean, symmetrical, hierarchical.

  trace any specific stock graphic or copy any real/trademarked team, MLB, college,
  or Day-of-the-Dead jersey logo.

SYSTEM / REDUCIBILITY:
- The primary emblem may carry rich detail, but the design SYSTEM must also yield a
  SIMPLIFIED ICON legible at 16–32px (a separate, much simpler derivative — e.g.
  the baseball + "11", or a minimal skull-ball glyph). When I ask for the icon,
  drop ornament and keep only what survives tiny.

OUTPUT DISCIPLINE:
- No spelling errors. Banner text spelled EXACTLY "MBM BASEBALL TRAINING".
- No stray watermarks, signatures, captions, or extra text inside the artwork (only
  cell labels when I ask for a labeled grid).
- For a grid: evenly-spaced, identically-treated labeled cells; change ONLY the one
  variable I name between cells.
- Use the background color I specify per prompt; keep each background 100% uniform.
- When I upload locked reference assets, reproduce them EXACTLY (form, proportion,
  color, letterforms, spelling); change only what I specify. Do not reinterpret a
  locked asset.
```
