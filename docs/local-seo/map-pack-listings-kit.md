## 1. Strategy — why this is winnable (and what it takes)

MBM is a **service-area business (SAB)**: Myles travels to fields, there's no storefront. Two consequences from 2026 local-search research:

1. **SABs rank harder on proximity.** With no fixed address, Google can't compute searcher-distance the way it does for storefronts, so MBM will rarely win pure "near me" proximity. ([rankai SAB guide](https://rankai.ai/articles/service-area-business-google-business-profile-guide), [Local University 2026](https://localu.org/live-10-signals-maps-2026/))
2. **So we win on the compensating signals** — and MBM is well-positioned on most:
   - **Reviews (the #2 Map Pack factor).** "200+ reviews @ 4.7 beats 15 reviews almost every time." This is MBM's single biggest gap and the top priority — see §4. ([localdominator](https://localdominator.co/local-search-ranking-factors/))
   - **Photos (a top signal; add fresh ones weekly).** ✅ Just solved — the new 28-shot golden-hour library is exactly this. Drip them into GBP weekly. ([localmighty](https://www.localmighty.com/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-best-practices/))
   - **Multi-city service areas + location pages + citations** (how SABs expand effective proximity). ✅ The site already has 6 location pages. ([localdominator](https://localdominator.co/local-search-ranking-factors/))
   - **Complete, accurate GBP** — GBP itself is **32% of Map Pack ranking; 8 of the top 10 signals come from the profile.** Fill in *everything*. ([advicelocal 2026](https://www.advicelocal.com/blog/2026-local-search-ranking-factors-maps-organic-ai/))

**Bottom line:** GBP completeness + a steady review flow + weekly photos are the three levers. Everything below sets up the first two; the photo library handles the third.

## 2. Google Business Profile — step by step

Create/manage at **business.google.com**.

### 2a. Business type (critical for SAB)
- When asked "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?" → **No**.
- Choose **"I deliver goods and services to my customers"** (service-area business). This **hides your street address** from the public while still letting you rank.
- Google still **requires a real address for verification** (Myles's home or home-base is fine — it stays private). **Do NOT use a PO Box or virtual office** — that violates Google guidelines.

### 2b. Categories
Pick the **most specific** baseball-coaching option Google autocompletes as your **primary**, then add secondaries. Recommended priority (use the closest match Google offers):

- **Primary (best available, in order):** `Baseball instructor` → `Sports coaching` / `Sports coaching service` → `Coaching center` → `Baseball club`
- **Secondary (add all that fit):** `Personal trainer`, `Sports school`, `Youth organization`

Primary category is a heavy ranking signal — choose the one a parent searching "baseball lessons" would expect.

## 4. Reviews — the #2 factor and MBM's biggest gap (do this from day one)

No genuine customer reviews exist yet (the site's "In His Own Words" quotes are Coach Myles's own cues, not testimonials). Reviews are the second-strongest Map Pack signal — so a steady flow matters more than any single optimization.

- After GBP verifies, Google generates a **short review link** (Profile → Ask for reviews). Save it.
- **Ask every happy family**, in person at the end of a session and via a follow-up text, with the direct link. The post-free-lesson moment is the best ask.
- Aim for **steady velocity** (a few per month beats a one-time batch) and a **4.7+** average.
- **Reply to every review** (engagement is itself a signal).
- ⚠️ **Honesty/policy:** real reviews only — never incentivize, gate, or self-author. (This is also why we are NOT adding `AggregateRating` markup to the website: there are no genuine collected reviews to mark up. Once real Google reviews accumulate, they show on Google directly.)
